## 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Penetration Tester and Security Researcher** specializing in Web Application, API, Network Security, and Red Team Operations.

I have hands-on experience discovering and reporting security vulnerabilities in organizations including **NASA, DHL Express, Nestlé, Amazon, Stanford University, MTM Group, and RMIT University** through bug bounty programs on HackerOne, Bugcrowd, and Intigriti.

I hold the **EC-Council Licensed Penetration Tester (LPT) Master** and **Certified Penetration Testing Professional (CPENT v2)** credentials. My interests include vulnerability research, adversary simulation, CTF challenges, and emerging security areas such as **Prompt Injection and LLM Security**.

I also lead **The Black Onion** red team, mentor junior security researchers, and contribute to cybersecurity communities through CTF organization, challenge development, and security education.
